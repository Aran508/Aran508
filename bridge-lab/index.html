How it is solved

Each member is a pin-jointed bar with axial stiffness EA. The solver uses XPBD with 60 substeps per frame, so member forces come straight from the constraint multipliers. A member fails when tension exceeds its yield load, or when compression exceeds the lower of yield and the Euler buckling load π²EI/L². Long members in compression buckle first.
